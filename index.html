FAQ

Questions companies and sales partners ask us.

Who is Build With People for?

Early-stage B2B companies that want to recruit, train, and manage independent, commission-based sales partners — and the sales partners who want to represent vetted products for a cut of what they sell.

How does company vetting work?

Before a company's opportunity goes live, we review the basics — what they're selling, who they're selling to, and the commission structure — so sales partners aren't walking into a vague or misleading offer.

What does "certified" mean for a sales partner?

Each company uploads its own sales material — pricing, positioning, objection handling. Partners study it and pass a quiz before they're approved to sell that specific offer.

How is commission tracked?

Closed deals are logged against the partner who closed them, so both sides can see exactly what's owed without a spreadsheet.

What does it cost?

Founding cohort companies use the platform free while we build it out together. See the pricing page for the current state of things.

Do you build websites, prototypes, or handle staffing?

No. Build With People is focused solely on helping companies recruit, train, and manage independent commission sales partners.
