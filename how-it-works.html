How Build With People works.

One platform, two sides: companies recruiting commission sales partners, and sales partners looking for a vetted product to represent.

For companies

From posting an opportunity to tracking commission.

Step 01

Post your opportunity

Describe your offer, your commission structure, and what a great partner looks like. We review it before it goes live.

Step 02

Train & certify partners

Upload sales materials, pricing, and FAQs. Applicants study and pass a certification quiz before they're approved to sell.

Step 03

Track every deal

See who's certified, who's active, and exactly what commission is owed as deals close.

For sales partners

From finding an offer to getting paid.

Step 01

Find an opportunity

Browse companies that are vetted before their opportunity goes live.

Step 02

Get certified

Study the company's sales packet and pass a certification quiz for that specific offer.

Step 03

Sell and get paid

Close deals, log them, and track exactly what commission you're owed.

Whichever side you're on, it starts on the homepage.
