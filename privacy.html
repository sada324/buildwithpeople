Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Build With People ("we", "us", "our") collects through the Platform, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, and password when you create an account.
  • Company information — details a Company submits about its business, opportunity, commission structure, and training material.
  • Sales partner information — profile details, certification results, and deal/commission activity logged on the Platform.
  • Usage information — basic analytics about how the Platform is used, to help us improve it.

2. How we use information

  • To operate core features: opportunity listings, training and certification, messaging, and commission tracking.
  • To vet Company listings before they're published.
  • To communicate with you about your account or activity on the Platform.
  • To improve the Platform.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing

Company and opportunity details you choose to publish are visible to other users as part of the Platform's core function. Sales Partner activity (such as certification status or deal history) is shared with the relevant Company as needed to operate that relationship. We may share information with service providers who help us run the Platform (such as hosting and database infrastructure), under obligations to protect it.

5. Data retention

We retain account and activity information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to operate the Platform and meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through the options on our homepage.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Platform evolves. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the contact options on our homepage.
